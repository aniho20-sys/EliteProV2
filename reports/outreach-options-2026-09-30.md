## A. Instagram 私訊英國獨立 PT

**第一步**：開 Instagram → 搜尋 `#ukpersonaltrainer` 或者 `#londonpt`（換成你附近城市）→ 揀 3–5 個**自己做、唔係連鎖健身室**、最近一星期有出帖、有講客人進度嘅 PT。

**私訊（copy 就用得）**：
> Hi [name] — I'm a PT too (I run my own studio). I got fed up tracking clients' session packs in notes and spreadsheets, so I built an app for it: programmes, bookings, session credits, invoices and client messages in one place. It's free for the first five coaches for three months — no card, no commission. Would you be up for trying it with a couple of your clients and telling me what's missing? Happy to send a link.

**佢哋常見反應 → 你嘅回覆**：
- **"How much is it?"** → "Nothing right now — the first five coaches get three months free, no card needed. If I ever charge after that I'll tell you well before, nothing is taken automatically."
- **"How many people use it?"** → "Honestly, it's early — right now it's me and my own nine clients. You'd be one of the first five coaches, which is exactly why I want your feedback."
- **"I already use Trainerize / TrueCoach."** → "Fair — the thing mine does that those don't is session packs: it deducts a session when a client books, handles late cancels, and shows both of you how many are left. If that's never a headache for you, it's probably not worth switching."
- **"Send the link."** → `https://elitepro-16718.web.app/#/landing`

**冇人覆**：唔好追問第二次。第二日揀另外 3–5 個。一星期發咗 20 個都冇人覆，就停，返嚟同 agent 改文案。

**完成標準**：一星期內私訊咗 20 個 PT。**成功標準**：最少 1 個人撳 link 註冊。

## B. 附近獨立健身室（自由 PT 租場嗰啲）

**第一步**：Google Maps 搜尋 `independent gym` 或者 `PT studio`（你附近）→ 揀 3 間**有自由 PT 租場**嘅（網站寫「PT rental」「freelance PTs welcome」嘅最啱）。

**Email 俾健身室老闆（copy 就用得）**：
> Subject: Free tool for the PTs who train at [gym name]
>
> Hi [name], I'm a personal trainer running my own studio nearby. I've built an app that handles the admin freelance PTs hate — session packs, bookings, late cancels and invoices — and it's free for the first five coaches for three months, no commission. Would you be happy to pass it on to the trainers who work from your gym, or let me put a small poster up? I've attached it. Thanks either way.

**海報（A5，agent 可以整成 PDF 俾你印）**：
> **Still tracking clients' sessions in your notes app?**
> Programmes, bookings, session packs and invoices — one app, built by a PT.
> First five coaches: three months free. No card. No commission.
> [QR code → landing page]

**佢哋常見反應 → 你嘅回覆**：
- **"We don't allow advertising."** → "No problem — would you mind just forwarding the email to your PTs? That's all."
- **"Are you trying to poach our trainers / clients?"** → "Not at all — it's only a tool for the trainers' own admin, their clients stay theirs and stay at your gym."

**冇人覆**：一星期後 email 一次（"Just checking this reached you"），之後唔再追。

**完成標準**：3 間健身室已收到 email 或者海報。

## C. PT 課程機構（新考牌嘅 PT）

**第一步**：Google 搜尋 `Level 3 personal trainer course UK` → 揀 5 間**細至中型**機構（大型連鎖通常唔覆）→ 喺網站搵 partnerships／contact email。

**Email（copy 就用得）**：
> Subject: Free business tool for your newly qualified PTs
>
> Hi — I'm a working PT and I've built an app for the part of the job courses don't cover: running your own clients. Programmes, bookings, session packs and invoices in one place. I'm offering it free for three months to the first coaches who try it, no commission. If you think it'd help your graduates, I'd be glad for you to mention it — no affiliate deal or anything expected. Link: https://elitepro-16718.web.app/#/landing

**佢哋常見反應 → 你嘅回覆**：
- **"What's in it for us?"** → "Honestly, just something useful to point new PTs at. If it helps, I'm happy to give your students a longer free period — let me know."（⚠️ 講之前要你決定係咪願意延長）
- **"Send more info."** → 回覆 landing page link。

**冇人覆**：唔追。課程機構通常唔覆，呢個選項本身就係「散網」。

**完成標準**：5 封 email 發出。
