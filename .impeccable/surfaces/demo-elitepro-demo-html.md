Scope: standalone design demo of two ElitePro screens (trainer dashboard, student training day). Demo only — the shipped app is not touched. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: independent trainer on a gym floor, and their student mid-workout, both one-handed on a phone. Bilingual EN / zh-HK chrome; training vocabulary stays English. Synthetic data, labelled.

Craft bar: Linear, Stripe Dashboard, Cal.com (user took the standing exit; convention is the commitment, executed straight).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A working list, not a wall of stat cards. Refuses the fitness-app grid of rings, gradient tiles and same-size icon cards; the day and the chase queue are the page.

OWN-WORLD: Near-monochrome graphite on off-white, mirrored to a designed near-black. The only chroma in the interface carries money or risk. Geist + Geist Mono, tabular slashed-zero figures, 1px hairline rules, 4px rhythm, ink-filled primary buttons, no decorative shadow.

STORY: The trainer reads today's sessions and exactly who needs chasing, with the reason stated, and acts in the row. The student sees today's work and logs a set without leaving the screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT (390px): date bar, one-line ledger strip (delivered / credits out / outstanding), then today's sessions as a time-ruled column with a now-line, then the attention queue with its reason column.

FORM: canon (standing exit), taken over grounded candidate 6 of 7; seed key 43650206.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: set commit to rest timer — checking a set locks its figures and raises a docked timer that counts a hairline rule down; trainer rows expand in place, never into a modal.

DISCIPLINES: absence is drawn (a zero balance is printed, not blank); staleness is printed (offline and last-synced are visible); a change holds its mark until acknowledged.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
